📋About This Course

National 5 Computing Science is assessed through two components: a question paper and a coursework assignment. Together these are worth 120 marks. The course covers four areas of study, with candidates choosing either Database Design & Development or Web Design & Development as their optional area.

This website covers: Software Design & Development, Computer Systems and Database Design & Development.

📚Course Areas

SDD
Mandatory

Software Design & Development

Design, implement, test and evaluate programs in Python. Covers the development process, design techniques, programming constructs and the three standard algorithms.

  • Iterative Development & Analysis
  • Design — (Wireframes, Structure Diagrams, Flowcharts & Pseudocode)
  • Data Types, Variables & 1-D Arrays
  • Input, Output & Calculations
  • Selection (if/elif) & Loops (Fixed/Conditional)
  • Pre-defined Functions — (random, round & length)
  • Standard Algorithms — (Input Validation, Running Total & Traversing an Array)
  • Testing & Evaluation

CS
Mandatory

Computer Systems

How data and instructions are stored in binary, basic computer architecture, the environmental impact of computing, and security precautions.

  • Binary Numbers & Floating-Point (Mantissa/Exponent)
  • Extended ASCII
  • Bitmap & Vector Graphics
  • Processor, Memory & Buses
  • Interpreters & Compilers
  • Environmental Impact
  • Firewalls & Encryption

DDD
Optional

Database Design & Development

Design and implement relational databases with two linked tables. Covers ER diagrams, data dictionaries, GDPR and SQL.

  • Fields, Records & Tables
  • End-user & Functional Requirements + UK GDPR
  • Primary/Foreign Keys & Referential Integrity
  • Data Dictionaries & Validation
  • ER Diagrams (One-to-Many)
  • SQL — SELECT, WHERE & ORDER BY
  • SQL — Equi-Joins, INSERT, UPDATE & DELETE
  • Testing & Evaluation
