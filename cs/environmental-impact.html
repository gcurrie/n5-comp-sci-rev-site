Introduction

Computers and technology can have an impact on the environment in a number of ways. Every computer left running consumes electricity — and generating that electricity usually produces carbon emissions. Multiply one school computer by the millions of machines running worldwide and the effect is enormous.

For National 5, you need to describe the energy use of computer systems, its implications for the environment, and how it can be reduced. The good news: it's mostly common sense!

Ways to Save Energy

Settings on monitors
Reduce the screen brightness, and set the monitor to switch off automatically after a period without use.
Power-down settings
Set the computer to power down at a certain time of day, or after a certain length of time unused — schools often shut every machine down overnight.
Standby
The monitor switches off and the hard drive usually stops spinning. Standby uses much less energy than full operation, without needing a complete restart — but it still uses some power, so switching off fully is better.
💡
There is usually a one or two-mark question on this in the National 5 assessments — any of the three points above will earn the marks.

Test Yourself

Q1 — Reducing energy use (2 marks)

A company is concerned about the environmental impact of its office computers. Describe two ways settings could be changed to reduce the energy the computers use.

Marking Instructions

Any two of: reduce monitor brightness / set monitors to switch off automatically when unused; set computers to power down after a set time or at a set time of day; use standby rather than leaving machines fully running (or switch off fully rather than standby). (1 mark each)

Q2 — Standby (1 mark)

Explain one drawback of leaving a computer on standby overnight rather than switching it off.

Marking Instructions

Standby still draws some power — over a whole night (or many machines) this wastes energy compared with switching off completely.
