Bitmap or Vector?

Ten images to sort. Decide how each one would be stored, not how it looks on the page — the exam asks the same thing, and the reasoning is always about what the picture is made of.

Show the two questions

Ask them in this order and stop at the first one that answers itself:

1 · Is it a photograph, a scan or a screenshot — lots of colours, no neat shapes?→ bitmap
2 · Is it built from clean shapes you could describe one at a time?→ vector

Watch for the clue words too. "array of pixels" means bitmap; "objects and attributes" means vector. Sometimes the question hands you the answer.
